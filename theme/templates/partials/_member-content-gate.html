{# _member-content-gate.html One definition of "may this visitor see member-only CONTENT", for the editor's "Members only" tickbox on an ordinary content module. WHY THIS EXISTS. Ten content modules each decided it for themselves, and all ten decided it the same wrong way: {% set _member_status = request_contact.individual_membership_status %} {% set _is_active_member = _member_status in ['Current'] %} That is the contact's OWN membership and nothing else, so a corporate employee - whose membership lives on their employer's company record and reaches them as the mirrored `wa_company_membership_*` properties - is not a member by this rule. It is Ref 72 in a different costume: the same population, the same cause (a narrower resolution than the one beside it), a different symptom. There it was the public price on a course card; here it is member content that never renders. Nothing shipped ticks that box today, which is the only reason this has not been reported. It is one tick in the page editor away from being live on any page, and whoever ticks it will have no way to tell: the page looks right to them, and to every member whose own contact record happens to carry a status. WHAT COUNTS. Any positive signal, on either record, with the CRM "no membership" sentinels scrubbed through `_member-tier.html` first (so "Not Applicable" on the contact falls through to the employer's tier rather than reading as a membership): - an active status on the contact, OR - an active status on the mirrored company, OR - a real membership tier on either. An explicitly lapsed / expired / cancelled status on EITHER record is a hard no and outranks all of it, matching the worker's gate (events-engine CLAUDE.md, "an explicitly lapsed/expired/cancelled status on either record is a hard no"). WHAT DOES NOT COUNT. A CAMS person id, and a membership expiry date. Both merely say this contact was migrated, not that they are a member, and counting them is precisely what "quoted every logged-in migrated contact a member rate they were then refused" (events-engine CLAUDE.md). A gate that says yes to every migrated contact is not a gate. COST. Mirror only - no `crm_object` call, no association sweep. Every property it reads is already on `request_contact`. That is deliberate: this runs on ordinary marketing pages where the tickbox is usually OFF, and the deeper ladders (`_member-company-resolve.html` for pricing, `_resource-member-resolve.html` for the Resources wall) are paid for by surfaces that need a TIER, not a yes/no. If a content gate ever needs to survive an employee with no mirrored company, that is the moment to include the company resolver here, not before. USAGE.
